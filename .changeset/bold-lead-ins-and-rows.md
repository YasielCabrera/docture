Weigh bold and monospace type by characters, so a bold lead-in such as `Coverage.` stays inside its paragraph instead of becoming a heading. A bold line at body size is a heading only when every run is bold, and bold table rows, amounts such as `Total due ₹17,348.36` or `Balance: 980.00`, rules, and lines without letters stay out of the outline. A bold version or section number such as `Version 3.11` is still a heading.
