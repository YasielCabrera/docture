"docture": minor
---

Join tables and paragraphs that continue across a page break. A table that ends in the bottom quarter of a page and continues at the top of the next page, in the same columns, cell count, font size, and weight, becomes one table on its starting page. A column whose cells are numbers rejects continuation rows with letters in it, and every continued row must start within three row spacings of the row above. A copy of its header row at the top of the next page is dropped from `rows` and kept in `sourceText`, and a two-row fragment that column detection would split is joined too. A paragraph that does not end with terminal punctuation, in the bottom quarter of its page, joins a next page that starts with a lowercase letter in its top quarter. The joined element records each later page in `ElementProvenance.continuations`, read with the new `provenanceSegments()`, and every detected table records `TableElement.rowProvenance`, one segment per row. Each page's content holds the elements that start on it, so the next page marker follows the joined body. A layout analyzer cannot retype, group, or move a joined element away from the end of its page's text. `parseDoctureDocument()` rejects continuations that skip pages or sit on other element kinds, and row provenance that does not match the rows.
